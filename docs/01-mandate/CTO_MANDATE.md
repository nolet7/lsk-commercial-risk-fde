# Mandate CRIP-001 | Fictional Meridian Global Bank

**Executive sponsor:** CTO. **Business owner:** Head of Financial Crime. **Delivery owner:** LSK FDE. **Status:** proposed, unsigned.

## Business mandate
Build an auditable, secure capability to screen corporate counterparties, trace ownership relationships, support analyst investigations, and deliver validated data products. Prove product/data fit before authorizing a pilot. Keep humans accountable for consequential decisions.

## Scope
In scope: customer technical scoping, synthetic evaluation, risk screening, corporate graph, REST interfaces, data pipelines, operational metrics, SOPs, and product feedback. Out of scope: definitive legal sanctions decisions, claims of proprietary Sayari access, unrestricted data sharing, automatic case approval, or a global risk judgment model.

## Proposed outcomes (subject to discovery)
- Improved investigation traceability; measurement from observed baseline to agreed pilot outcome.
- 100% required audit fields for completed decisions.
- Validated export reconciliation and explicit failure handling.
- Defined service SLOs with customer-owned escalation.

## Gate G0
CTO authorizes only time-boxed discovery with synthetic fixtures until data-use agreements, sponsor and information-security approvals are recorded.
