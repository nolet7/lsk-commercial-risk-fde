# Senior FDE interview defense

## Mandate → delivery narrative
Explain why the first output was a scoped workflow and not a graph database; how evaluation used an independently labeled golden set; why indirect ownership arithmetic is separate from legal sanctions rules; why durable async jobs and reconciliation matter; and how SLOs tie to lost customer trust.

## Five whiteboard challenges
1. A CTO wants AI to automate all compliance decisions. Show responsible limitations and a staged evaluation.
2. A company is owned through three intermediate entities. Demonstrate graph direction, temporal evidence, aggregate ownership complexity and manual review.
3. Millions of records need daily Parquet export. Explain Glue/Spark, partitioning, manifests and Airflow recovery.
4. Provider 429s trigger backlog while the last dataset is stale. Explain throttling, idempotency, status and customer communication.
5. Three clients report the same alias-matching issue. Create a reusable core engineering issue with impact, reproduction, proposed contract and verification.
